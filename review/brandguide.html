The mark

Three things carry the brand, and they always appear together in the monogram: the A and V, set in Cinzel and overlapped so they read as one shape; the mountain ridge, a single gold line rising from the A's apex; and the peacock feather, whose quill follows the V's right stroke.

The feather is the only ornament. It carries the one flash of colour in the identity and it earns that by being the only thing in the mark that is not a letter or a line. Everything else is cream, gold and the deep forest green the whole system sits on.

Cinzel gives the name its Roman capitals. Montserrat carries IMMOBILIEN and the tagline, letterspaced wide so the two typefaces never compete.

The one rule that matters. The logo is artwork, not a layout. Place it, scale it proportionally, and leave it alone.

Clear space and minimum size

Clear space

Keep a margin of X on all four sides, where X is the cap height of the A in AUGUSTE. Nothing enters that band: no type, no rule, no photo edge, no other logo. Every supplied file already has X baked into its viewBox, so placing the file flush against a box still leaves the margin standing.

Use for IMMOBILIEN, the tagline, body copy, tables, captions, the e-mail signature and every interface label.

Imagery

Photography is calm, wide and unpeopled. Architecture at golden hour, deep green planting, soft haze on distance. Grade towards forest green, cream and gold; pull saturation down rather than up.

Always leave one third of the frame quiet for type.
Never a stock-looking handshake, a key close-up, or a sky oversaturated into cyan.
Watermark with the monogram at 9 % opacity, bottom-right, never across the subject.
